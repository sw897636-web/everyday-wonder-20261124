Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3): one sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new.

## The sentence

> The elevator mirror catches me mid-yawn, and for one floor I am a stranger borrowing my face.

## Run

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the sentence deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`, falling back to `./output/entry.txt` when `/output` is not writable:

```sh
node main.ts
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output. The sentence is 93 characters, a single sentence.
